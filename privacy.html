1. Overview

Travis Guardian ("we", "us", "our") builds offline-first financial software for Kenya and East Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our website at travisguardian.org and our flagship application, TRAVIS (together, the "Services").

The short version: TRAVIS is 100% offline. Your financial data never leaves your device. We do not collect, store, or share your financial data.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the absolute minimum required to run our Services:

a) Website visitors

  • No tracking cookies. Our company website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
  • Server logs. Our hosting providers (such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare) may keep standard access logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) for security and uptime. We do not access or analyze these logs beyond security incidents.

b) TRAVIS app users

  • Financial data. All transactions, budgets, accounts, and reports you enter are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see them, and they are never transmitted to us.
  • Device fingerprint (activation only). When you first install TRAVIS, the app generates a one-way cryptographic hash of your device. This hash is sent once, at activation, so we can issue your activation code. It contains no personal or financial data and cannot be reversed.
  • No account required. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or bank login to use TRAVIS.

c) Communication

If you contact us via email (support@, sales@, careers@, or hello@), we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use this only to reply to you.

3. How We Use Data

We use the limited data we collect only for:

  • Issuing and validating TRAVIS activation codes
  • Responding to your support, sales, or career inquiries
  • Protecting against abuse, fraud, or unauthorized distribution of TRAVIS
  • Complying with legal obligations under Kenyan law

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Storage

TRAVIS stores all user data in an encrypted local database on your device. If you uninstall TRAVIS or clear its data, that data is permanently deleted — there is no hidden backup on our side.

Activation records (the one-way device hash and its activation status) may be retained on our server so we can validate licenses. These records contain no personal or financial information.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We disclose information only:

  • When required by a valid legal order under Kenyan law
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Travis Guardian, our users, or the public
  • To service providers who help us operate (for example, email hosting) under strict confidentiality

6. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (limited to communication records)
  • Request correction or deletion of that data
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)

Because your financial data lives only on your device, you already control it directly. To exercise rights over communication data, email us at privacy@travisguardian.org.

7. Children's Privacy

TRAVIS is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be announced on our website.
