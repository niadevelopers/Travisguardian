Activation

Why TRAVIS uses device activation.

A one-time, privacy-respecting way to license your copy of TRAVIS.

Your device, your license.

When you first install TRAVIS, the app generates a device fingerprint — a one-way hash that identifies your installation. This hash is sent to our server only once, at activation, so an administrator can issue your activation code.

After activation, TRAVIS works entirely offline. No further communication with our servers is needed — ever.

  • No personal data is included in the fingerprint
  • No financial data is ever transmitted
  • Fingerprint is a one-way hash — it cannot be reversed
  • Used only to issue and validate your license
  • After activation, TRAVIS is fully offline
