About Travis Guardian

A financial intelligence company, born in Kenya.

We build offline-first software that gives every Kenyan — from a market stall to a growing enterprise — the clarity, control, and confidence to master their money.

Our Story

It started with a simple question.

"Where did my money go?"

That question — asked by a shop owner in Nairobi, a teacher in Mombasa, a matatu operator in Thika — is what Travis Guardian was built to answer. Across Kenya, millions of people earn, spend, and save every day without ever knowing whether they're actually making progress.

Existing tools were either built for accountants (too complex), built for the cloud (too expensive, too fragile offline), or built for the West (too generic). None of them understood M-Pesa. None of them worked in Kisumu without signal. None of them spoke plain Swahili.

So we built TRAVIS — a financial intelligence system that lives entirely on your device, costs a one-time fee, and advises you like a personal CFO. No internet. No monthly bills. No accounting degree required.

Mission & Vision

Why we exist.

Two commitments guide everything we build at Travis Guardian.

🎯

Our Mission

To give every Kenyan — regardless of income, location, or education — the financial clarity and intelligence that was once reserved for corporations with CFOs.

🔭

Our Vision

Our Promise

Your data stays on your device. We will never sell it, store it, or require an internet connection to use what you paid for.

Our Values

The principles behind every line of code.

These are not slogans. They are the rules we build by.

📵

Offline-first, always

Connectivity is a privilege, not a guarantee. Everything we build must work without it — in Kisumu, in Meru, on a matatu, anywhere.

🔒

Privacy is non-negotiable

Your finances are your business. We don't collect, transmit, or sell your data. It lives on your device — and stays there.

🇰🇪

Built for Kenya, not adapted

We design for M-Pesa, chamas, irregular income, and rural reality — not the other way around.

🗣️

Plain language over jargon

No one should need an accounting degree to understand their own money. We explain everything in the language you speak.

⚡

One-time, lifetime, no strings

No subscriptions. No upsells. No hidden fees. You buy it once and it's yours — including future updates.

🤝

Built with the community

Shop owners, teachers, farmers, and developers shape what we build. Our users are our co-founders.

Our Journey

From an idea to a movement.

Key milestones in the Travis Guardian story.

Early 2025
The problem takes shape
Research begins. Conversations with shop owners, teachers, and SMEs across Kenya reveal the same pain: no one knows where their money actually goes.
Mid 2025
Travis Guardian is founded
A small team of Kenyan developers commits to building offline-first financial software that speaks plain language and respects privacy.
Late 2025
TRAVIS Financial Core v1.0
The first version of TRAVIS ships with double-entry accounting, M-Pesa tracking, AI advisory, and 100% offline capability. Live at travis-three.vercel.app.
Today
Growing across East Africa
TRAVIS is now used by hundreds of Kenyans — shop owners, professionals, families, and chamas — with the same commitment: offline, private, one-time purchase.
Next
The future of financial intelligence
Expanding TRAVIS to more East African markets, adding deeper AI forecasting, and building a financial literacy movement that reaches every county.
