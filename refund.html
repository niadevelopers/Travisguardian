Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026 · Effective immediately

Contents

1. Summary 2. Eligibility 3. Not Eligible 4. How to Request 5. Processing Time 6. Disputes 7. Contact

1. Summary

We want you to be satisfied with TRAVIS. Because TRAVIS is a digital product delivered instantly and activated to your device, refunds are limited — but we're committed to resolving any issues you experience fairly.

In short: You may request a refund within 7 days of purchase only if your activation code was never issued or the app fails to function despite our support team's help.

2. Refund Eligibility

You may be eligible for a refund if:

Situation Refund? Conditions
Paid but never received an activation code ✔ Yes Within 7 days of payment, after contacting support
App won't run on your device despite support's help ✔ Yes Within 7 days of purchase, after attempting support resolution
Duplicate payment (paid twice by mistake) ✔ Yes Reported within 7 days — refund for the duplicate amount
Accidental purchase by a minor ✔ Yes Reported within 7 days with proof

3. Situations Not Eligible for Refund

We cannot offer refunds in these cases:

Situation Refund?
You used TRAVIS for more than 7 days✘ No
You changed your mind after using the app✘ No
You entered inaccurate financial data and want your money back✘ No
You lost your device or deleted the app✘ No
You disagree with the AI advisor's suggestions✘ No
You attempted to bypass activation or violate our Terms✘ No

4. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you're eligible, follow these steps:

  1. Email support@travisguardian.org with the subject line "Refund Request".
  2. Include your payment confirmation (M-Pesa code, bank receipt, or transaction ID), the phone number or email used for payment, and the date of purchase.
  3. Briefly explain why you're requesting the refund.
  4. Our team will review your request and respond within 3 business days.
  5. If approved, refunds are sent to the same M-Pesa number or bank account used for payment.

5. Processing Time

  • Review: up to 3 business days
  • Approval to payout: 3–7 business days
  • M-Pesa refunds: typically instant after approval
  • Bank refunds: 3–5 business days depending on your bank

6. Disputes

If you're not satisfied with our decision, you may escalate the matter by emailing legal@travisguardian.org. Disputes are governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya, and we encourage amicable resolution before any formal proceedings.

7. Contact

  • Refund requests: support@travisguardian.org
  • Legal escalation: legal@travisguardian.org
  • Location: Nairobi, Kenya 🇰🇪
