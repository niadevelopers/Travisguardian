FAQ

Everything you need to know before you buy.

Is KES 999 really a one-time payment?

Yes. You pay KES 999 once and get lifetime access to TRAVIS — including all future updates. There are no monthly fees, no renewal charges, and no hidden costs.

What happens if I change my phone or computer?

Your data is stored locally on your device. You can back up your TRAVIS data file and restore it on a new device — no cloud account required. Contact support for help migrating.

Does TRAVIS work without internet?

Yes — 100%. TRAVIS was built offline-first. You don't need mobile data, WiFi, or any connection to use every feature, including AI advice and forecasting.

Do I need accounting knowledge to use it?

No. TRAVIS handles double-entry accounting behind the scenes and explains everything in plain language — English or Swahili. If you can record a transaction, you can use TRAVIS.

Is my financial data safe?

Your data never leaves your device. We don't store it on our servers, we don't have access to it, and we don't share it with anyone. You own 100% of your financial data.

Can I use TRAVIS for both business and personal finances?

Yes. TRAVIS includes two independent modules — Business and Personal — so your business and personal finances stay separate and clear, without needing two apps.

What devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, Windows laptops and PCs, Mac computers, and any browser-capable device. It works everywhere you do.

Do you offer refunds?

Please see our Refund Policy for details. Because TRAVIS is a digital product delivered instantly, refunds are limited — but we're committed to resolving any issues you experience.

How do I activate TRAVIS after paying?

After payment you'll receive an activation code from our team. Enter it during first launch to unlock lifetime access on your device.
